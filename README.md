# Japanese vs. American web design

A simple side-by-side comparison of the Japanese and US homepages of Starbucks and 7-Eleven. Pick a row in the table to see where each difference shows up on both pages.

Static site: `index.html` and the screenshots in `shots/`. No build step.

## Sources
- [Differences Between American and Japanese UX Design](https://blog.btrax.com/american-japanese-ux-design/), btrax, 2019
- [Japanese Website Design Trends](https://blog.btrax.com/japan-web-design-localization/), btrax, 2019

Screenshots of starbucks.co.jp, starbucks.com, sej.co.jp and 7-eleven.com were captured on November 14, 2026. The sites belong to their owners and may have changed since.
